## 11-2 题1(2)

求解 $(xy^3-2x^4)z_x+(2y^4-x^3y)z_y=9z(x^3-y^3)$.

### 解答 11-2 题1(2)

首次积分 $x^2y^2/(x^3+y^3)$ 与 $(x^3+y^3)^3z^2$. $\blacksquare$

## 11-2 题2

求解 $\sqrt{x}f_x+\sqrt{y}f_y+\sqrt{z}f_z=f$, $f(x,1,z)=xz$.

### 解答 11-2 题2

$f=e^{2\sqrt{y}-2}(\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y}+1)^2(1-\sqrt{y}+\sqrt{z})^2$. $\blacksquare$
