> **Basic Knowledge: Exact Differential Equation**<br>If $\mathrm d\Phi=P\mathrm dx+Q\mathrm dy$, then $P\mathrm dx+Q\mathrm dy=0$ is exact, with general integral $\Phi=C$.

> **Theorem 2.1 (Criterion and Formula for Exact Equations)**<br>$P\mathrm dx+Q\mathrm dy=0$ is exact iff $\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}$.

## 2-1 Problem 3

Solve $\left(ax+by\right)\mathrm dx+\left(bx+cy\right)\mathrm dy=0$.

### Solution 2-1 Problem 3

$\frac a2x^2+bxy+\frac c2y^2=C$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-1 Problem 6

Solve $\left(ye^x+2e^x+y^2\right)\mathrm dx+\left(e^x+2xy\right)\mathrm dy=0$.

### Solution 2-1 Problem 6

$e^x\left(y+2\right)+xy^2=C$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-1 Problem 9

Solve $\frac{2s-1}{t}\mathrm ds+\frac{s-s^2}{t^2}\mathrm dt=0$.

### Solution 2-1 Problem 9

$\frac{s^2-s}{t}=C$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-1 Problem 10

Solve $xf\left(x^2+y^2\right)\mathrm dx+yf\left(x^2+y^2\right)\mathrm dy=0$.

### Solution 2-1 Problem 10

$\frac12F\left(x^2+y^2\right)=C$, where $F'=f$. $\blacksquare$
