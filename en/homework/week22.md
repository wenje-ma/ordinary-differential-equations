## 7-2 Problem 3

Solve Hermite's equation $y''-2xy'+\lambda y=0$.

### Solution 7-2 Problem 3

$a_{n+2}=\frac{2n-\lambda}{(n+2)(n+1)}a_n$. $\blacksquare$

## 7-2 Problem 4

Find two power series solutions of $y''+(\sin x)y=0$ at $x=0$.

### Solution 7-2 Problem 4

$y_1=1-\frac{x^3}6+\cdots$, $y_2=x-\frac{x^4}{12}+\cdots$. $\blacksquare$
