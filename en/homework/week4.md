> **Basic Knowledge: First-Order Linear Equation**<br>$\frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}+p\left(x\right)y=q\left(x\right)$.

> **Basic Knowledge: Homogeneous Solution**<br>$y=Ce^{-\int p\mathrm dx}$.

> **Basic Knowledge: Nonhomogeneous Solution (Integrating Factor)**<br>$y=e^{-\int p\mathrm dx}\left[\int q e^{\int p\mathrm dx}\mathrm dx+C\right]$.

## 2-3 Problem 1(2)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}+y\tan x=\sin2x$.

### Solution 2-3 Problem 1(2)

$y=C\cos x-2\cos^2x$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-3 Problem 1(4)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}-\frac1{1-x^2}y=1+x$, $y\left(0\right)=1$.

### Solution 2-3 Problem 1(4)

$y=\sqrt{\frac{1+x}{1-x}}\left[1+\frac12\left(x\sqrt{1-x^2}+\arcsin x\right)\right]$. $\blacksquare$
