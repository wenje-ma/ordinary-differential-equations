## 5-1 Problem 1

Estimate $g$ using the linear pendulum.

### Solution 5-1 Problem 1

$g=\frac{4\pi^2l}{T^2}$. $\blacksquare$

## 5-1 Problem 2

Prove nonlinear pendulum is isochronous not and can precess.

### Solution 5-1 Problem 2

By first integral, $T\left(A\right)>\frac{2\pi}a$ and increases with $A$. $\blacksquare$
