## 11-1 Problem 1(1)

Solve $\sum x_i\partial y/\partial x_i=0$.

### Solution 11-1 Problem 1(1)

$y=\psi\left(x_1/x_n,\cdots\right)$. $\blacksquare$

## 11-1 Problem 1(3)

Solve the 3-variable PDE.

### Solution 11-1 Problem 1(3)

$h=\psi\left(a^2-b^2+c^2,a^2/\left(b^2c^2\right)\right)$. $\blacksquare$

## 11-1 Problem 2(2)

Solve the IVP.

### Solution 11-1 Problem 2(2)

$z=\psi\left(x/y+y\right)$. $\blacksquare$
