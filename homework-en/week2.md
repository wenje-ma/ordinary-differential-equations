> **Theorem 2.1 (Criterion and Formula for Exact Equations)**
> 
> $P\mathrm{d}x+Q\mathrm{d}y=0$ is exact iff $\frac{\partial{P}}{\partial{y}}=\frac{\partial{Q}}{\partial{x}}$.

## 2-1 Problem 10

Solve $xf\left(x^2+y^2\right)\mathrm{d}x+yf\left(x^2+y^2\right)\mathrm{d}y=0$.

### Solution to 2-1 Problem 10

$\frac12F\left(x^2+y^2\right)=C$, where $F'=f$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-2 Problem 1(3)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}+y^2\sin{x}=0$.

### Solution to 2-2 Problem 1(3)

$y=-\frac1{\cos{x}+C}$, plus $y\equiv0$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-2 Problem 1(5)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}=\left(\cos{x}\cos2y\right)^2$.

### Solution to 2-2 Problem 1(5)

$\tan2y=x+\frac12\sin2x+C$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-2 Problem 2(2)

Solve $x\mathrm{d}x+ye^{-x}\mathrm{d}y=0$, $y\left(0\right)=1$.

### Solution to 2-2 Problem 2(2)

$y^2=2e^x\left(1-x\right)-1$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-2 Problem 2(4)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}=\frac{\ln\left|x\right|}{1+y^2}$, $y\left(1\right)=0$.

### Solution to 2-2 Problem 2(4)

$y+\frac{y^3}3=x\ln\left|x\right|-x+1$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-2 Problem 3(2)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}=ay$.

### Solution to 2-2 Problem 3(2)

$y=C_1e^{ax}$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-2 Problem 3(4)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}=y^n$.

### Solution to 2-2 Problem 3(4)

$n=\frac13$: $y=\pm\left[\frac23\left(x+C\right)\right]^{3/2}$; $n=1$: $y=Ce^x$; $n=2$: $y=-\frac1{x+C}$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-2 Problem 5

Prove local uniqueness on $y=a$ iff the improper integral diverges.

### Solution to 2-2 Problem 5

By separation of variables. $\blacksquare$

## 2-3 Problem 1(2)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}+y\tan{x}=\sin2x$.

### Solution to 2-3 Problem 1(2)

$y=C\cos{x}-2\cos^2x$. $\blacksquare$
