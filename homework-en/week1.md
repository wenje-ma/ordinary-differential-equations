> **Definition 1.2 (Solution)**
> 
> A function $\varphi$ such that substituting it and its derivatives into the equation yields an identity.

> **Theorem 2.1 (Criterion and Formula for Exact Equations)**
> 
> $P\mathrm dx+Q\mathrm dy=0$ is exact iff $\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}$.

## 1-1 Problem 1(4)

Verify that the given piecewise function solves $y'=\sqrt{\left|y\right|}$.

### Solution to 1-1 Problem 1(4)

By checking each of the three intervals. $\blacksquare$

## 1-2 Problem 2(3)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm dR}{\mathrm dt}=-aR$, $R\left(0\right)=1$.

### Solution to 1-2 Problem 2(3)

$R\left(t\right)=e^{-at}$. $\blacksquare$

## 1-2 Problem 2(4)

Solve $\frac{\mathrm dy}{\mathrm dx}=1+y^2$, $y\left(x_0\right)=y_0$.

### Solution to 1-2 Problem 2(4)

$y=\tan\left(x+\arctan y_0-x_0\right)$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-1 Problem 3

Solve $\left(ax+by\right)\mathrm dx+\left(bx+cy\right)\mathrm dy=0$.

### Solution to 2-1 Problem 3

$\frac a2x^2+bxy+\frac c2y^2=C$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-1 Problem 6

Solve $\left(ye^x+2e^x+y^2\right)\mathrm dx+\left(e^x+2xy\right)\mathrm dy=0$.

### Solution to 2-1 Problem 6

$e^x\left(y+2\right)+xy^2=C$. $\blacksquare$

## 2-1 Problem 9

Solve $\frac{2s-1}{t}\mathrm ds+\frac{s-s^2}{t^2}\mathrm dt=0$.

### Solution to 2-1 Problem 9

$\frac{s^2-s}{t}=C$. $\blacksquare$
