## 6-3 Problem 3

(1) Prove Wronskian is constant for $y''+qy=0$. (2) Find solution given $y=e^x$.

### Solution 6-3 Problem 3

(1) By Liouville's formula. (2) $q=-1$, $y=C_1e^x+C_2e^{-x}$. $\blacksquare$
